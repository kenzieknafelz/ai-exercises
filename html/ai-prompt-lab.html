AI Safety Tips

  • Always verify AI-generated information against trusted sources.
  • Never share passwords or personal data with an AI chatbot.
  • Use AI as a starting point - human review is still important.
  • Be aware that AI can reflect real-world biases from its training data.
  • AI can produce confident-sounding but incorrect answers (hallucinations).
